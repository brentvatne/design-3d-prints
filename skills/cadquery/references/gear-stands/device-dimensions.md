# Device Dimensions Reference

Comprehensive specifications for electronic music devices commonly targeted by 3D printed stands.

## Elektron

All Elektron "small box" devices share the same form factor:

### Digitakt / Digitakt II / Digitone / Digitone II / Syntakt / Analog Heat
| Specification | Value |
|---------------|-------|
| Width | 215 mm |
| Depth | 176 mm |
| Height | 63 mm (including knobs) |
| Weight | 1.45-1.48 kg |
| VESA Mounting | 100 × 100 mm pattern |
| Screw Type | M4 |
| **Max Screw Length** | **7 mm** (CRITICAL - longer hits PCB!) |

## Roland Boutique Series

All Boutique modules share width and depth:

| Model | Width | Depth | Height | Weight |
|-------|-------|-------|--------|--------|
| JP-08 | 300 mm | 128 mm | 46 mm | 970 g |
| JU-06A | 300 mm | 128 mm | 49 mm | 995 g |
| JD-08 | 300 mm | 128 mm | 49 mm | ~1 kg |
| JX-08 | 300 mm | 128 mm | 49 mm | ~1 kg |
| TR-06 | 300 mm | 128 mm | 46 mm | ~970 g |
| TR-08 | 300 mm | 128 mm | 46 mm | ~970 g |
| SH-01A | 300 mm | 128 mm | 49 mm | ~1 kg |
| **SE-02** | 300 mm | 128 mm | **52 mm** | ~1 kg |

**Note**: SE-02 is taller than other Boutiques - may not fit all stands!
